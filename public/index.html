      How scoring works

      Per activity

      Every item has its own drop rate for each boss or raid mode. Guaranteed drops, kill-count capes and items that also drop elsewhere (whips, pickaxes, godsword shards, Virtus and similar) are shown but never counted as luck. Rarer items count for more than common ones.

      Your haul is compared with 20,000 simulated players who made the same number of kills. The percentile says how many of them would have done worse than you: 50 is average, 97.5 and above is spooned, below 2.5 is very dry.

      Overall

      Each activity's luck is standardised and averaged, weighted by how much information it carries and how reliable its drop data is. The score is that average placed on a 0–100 scale.

      Real accounts run luckier than the maths predicts, partly because people stop farming once they get the drop. The scale allows for that, so a typical account lands near 50. Treat scores as a guide, not a verdict.

      Data

      Kill counts and collection logs come from TempleOSRS, so update your profile there first. Drop rates come from the OSRS Wiki. Activities tagged partial or rough have less certain rates.
