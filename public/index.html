How it works

  • Every item has its own per-kill rate (per mode/source for raids and multi-boss activities). Guaranteed drops and KC-based capes are shown but never counted as luck.
  • Rarer items count for more: each item is weighted by its expected wait in kills.
  • Your haul is compared with 20,000 simulated players with your exact KC. Percentile 50 is average; high is spooned, low is dry.
  • Items with shared pools (Hydra pieces, Araxxor, Abyssal Sire, Vestiges, pity timers) are simulated as groups, not independent drops.
  • Overall score = −10 × combined z-score, so lower is more spooned. Activities with only approximate data are excluded unless you tick the box.
  • Rates were collected from the OSRS Wiki on 2026-10-12. Items marked partial/approx may be off; see README.md.
